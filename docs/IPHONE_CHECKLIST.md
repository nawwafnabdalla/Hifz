# iPhone check: the new practice flow, offline audio, your progress

## New practice flow (this release)
1. Learn an ayah from Ta-Ha: one screen, audio bar above **Said it**. Play it, say it aloud, tap. The next step shows only the first word; the rest is grey bars. Say it from the first word and tap; then recall. Does the first-word step read clearly in the Madinah font?
2. On the faded step, tap **Show me**: the text returns. Do it twice on one ayah and learn it again another day: the app should offer to repair that joint.
3. Leave mid-ayah and return: you land on the same step.

## Offline audio and progress

**Verified on a real iPhone so far: nothing in this release.** Please tell me what you see.

1. **Save Ta-Ha audio.** First, once: Settings → About → Audio sources & checks → *Check my three reciters*; Maher's **Offline saving** line should be ✓.
   Then Today → your Ta-Ha goal (Maher as reciter) → *Save audio*. You should see "Saving 12 of 36 · 2.4 MB" counting up, then "Ready offline · N MB". If some couldn't be saved, tell me the message.
2. **Play offline.** Switch on airplane mode, open Ta-Ha, press play (try Echo too). The line under the player says "saved offline".
3. **Delete audio.** Settings → *Offline recitations* (near the top). The size matches what you saw. Tap **Remove audio** (or **Remove all downloads**); it says your progress stays saved.
4. **Progress intact.** Back on Today and Progress: your goals, recalled counts and next reviews are exactly as before, and Settings still says Synced.

Yasser and Badr stream as before but can't be saved (the card says so and offers Maher).
